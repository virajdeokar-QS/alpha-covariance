Model boundaries: The scatter plot uses deterministic synthetic observations; its visual sample statistics need not equal the exact input correlation. Portfolio volatility is computed analytically from the formula, not estimated from the scatter. No forecasts, returns, recommendations or real market data are used. Risk is not fully captured by volatility.

WHY THIS EXISTS

An instrument should make the assumptions legible.

Correlation measures co-movement, not causality. Covariance depends on units and volatilities. The portfolio volatility result depends on a fixed covariance matrix assumption; in real markets both volatility and correlation can shift. This is a public teaching interface, not a trading system.
