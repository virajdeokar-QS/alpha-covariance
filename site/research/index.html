OUR MINIMUM STANDARD

No standalone Sharpe ratio. No backtest without its assumptions.

Research must explain the sample, sources, processing, timing assumptions, validation design, transaction costs where relevant, and the circumstances under which a finding might fail. A reported result remains provisional until reproduced from a documented workflow.

01

Provenance

Where the data came from, when it was available and what transformations were made.

02

Validation

Out-of-sample evaluation, sensitivity checks and guardrails against leakage.

03

Interpretation

Limitations, alternative explanations and the difference between evidence and speculation.
