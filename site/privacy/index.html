SITE DATA

Website usage: The public pages do not require an account and do not install custom analytics cookies. Google Fonts are requested from Google when online, which may receive technical connection data. Our hosting provider may process technical connection and security data according to its own policy.

Interactive lab: Values entered into the covariance playground are processed in your browser. They are not sent to our server or stored as a research record.

Inquiries: When the optional secure form is activated, we collect the name, email, topic and message you submit, with your consent, solely to handle the inquiry. Inquiries are stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database accessible only to authorised site administrators. Cloudflare Turnstile processes security challenge information.

Retention: Inquiry data should be reviewed and deleted when no longer needed; an automated retention schedule has not yet been implemented. Do not submit passwords, bank details, portfolio statements or other sensitive information.

Private Research Desk: Approved sign-ins are processed through Cloudflare Access, which handles account identifiers and security events. Authenticated research owners may save strategy specifications, revisions and audit events in private Cloudflare D1 storage. Approved, expiring external reviewers may view owner-selected research specifications and submit attributed assessments; reviewer email, appointment expiry and feedback are recorded in the private D1 database. Passwords are not stored in research tables; Cloudflare Access verifies sign-in identity. When the optional local research engine is connected, it stores owner-imported, rights-declared historical datasets, backtest records, virtual orders, fills and paper-wallet audit events in a local SQLite database and protected files on the owner-controlled computer. No broker orders or customer funds are processed. The engine may be unavailable when that computer is offline.

Private data retention: Strategy, experiment and local paper records remain until the operator deletes them or the storage is retired. A fully automated retention/deletion service is not yet in place. Access logs may also be retained by hosting and identity providers according to their policies. Contact research@alphacovariance.com with access or deletion requests.

Contact: Privacy and deletion requests can be sent to research@alphacovariance.com. The statement will be reviewed as the site and its data practices develop.
