03 / VALIDATION POLICY

A hypothesis is not an alpha.

Research drafts are not trading signals. Moving a strategy toward validation will require historical-data provenance, out-of-sample testing, transaction-cost models, reproducible run IDs, uncertainty estimates and signed research approval. An experimental long-only historical backtest and virtual paper-replay engine is available in the separate, owner-only Research Operations Lab when its local service and licensed historical datasets are connected. This does not constitute statistical alpha validation or live market execution.
